Engineered specifically for high-resolution desktop publishing, literature, newspaper columns, posters, and digital web typography, Dubori Asomiya Serif bridges classical Vaishnavite literary heritage with state-of-the-art OpenType digital typography.

Key features of the typeface family:

  • 11 Typographic Styles: Includes Regular (400), Medium (500), SemiBold (600), Bold (700), and heavy Black (900), paired with dedicated 75% narrow Condensed styles (Condensed, Bold Condensed, Black Condensed) for tight newspaper columns, alongside 11.5° cursive slants (Italic, Bold Italic), and a continuous Variable Font format supporting weight (wght) and width (wdth) interpolation axes.
  • Native Assamese Script Optimization: Injects explicit OpenType LangSys 'ASM ' declarations into both bng2 and beng layout tables, strictly preserving authentic Assamese Ra (ৰ - U+09F0), Va (ৱ - U+09F1), and precomposed Ya (য় - U+09DF).

  • Complete 209-Conjunct Shaper: Thoroughly verified with the HarfBuzz shaping engine across all 209 authentic compound characters (যুক্তাক্ষৰ), including rare historical Vaishnavite Brajavali ligatures.
